The learning center's base is at [Regen Tropics](https://regentropics.com/), 36 Kunkle St, Oak View, California from where we will make frequent field trips to other spaces in and around Ojai.

We start operating with a maximum of 15 children, 4-18 yrs, in a multi-age learning environment with a minimum 3 day/week commitment.

- 9am: Arrivals & Circle Time
- 12pm: Lunch time
- 3:00pm: Circle Time
- 3:00pm: End of Day

Daily activities include:

- Circle time: movement, singing, dancing, noticing, sitting quietly, announcements, wonderings, and sharing.
- Exploring phenomena: interdisciplinary explorations of primary soruces, materials, and phenomena in diverse subject matters - *from math to dance*.
- Dialogues & Inquiry: dialogues rooted in core questions of the week/month.
- Independent Learning: time for self-directed reading, writing, drawing, movement, making, and other explorations responding to the child’s individual explorations.
- Reflection Time: journaling, drawing, and group sharing.
- Walks and Hikes

*The root meaning of ‘school’ originates from leisure.* The structure will prioritize a sense of leisure.
